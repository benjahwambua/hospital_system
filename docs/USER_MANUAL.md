# HMS User Manual

## 1. Introduction

This manual explains how operational users use the Hospitalis Hospital Management System.

Your available menus depend on the access rights assigned to your HMS account. If a menu is not visible or an operation returns **403 Forbidden**, contact the HMS administrator.

## 2. Signing in

1. Open HMS in the browser.
2. Enter your assigned username/login credentials.
3. Sign in.
4. Confirm that your name and assigned role are displayed in the sidebar.
5. Use only your own account. Do not share passwords or accounts.

## 3. Understanding the sidebar

HMS displays only modules available to your account.

Main areas are:

- Front Desk
- Clinical
- Laboratory
- Radiology
- Pharmacy
- Maternity
- Finance & Billing
- Procurement
- Finance
- Administration (super-user area)
- Staff

The exact actions available within a module also depend on your View/Create/Edit/Delete/Approve rights.

## 4. Reception: registering a patient

1. Open **Reception**.
2. Select **Register Patient**.
3. Enter the patient's required demographic information.
4. Select the appropriate clinical department/service.
5. For an ANC/maternity workflow, select the appropriate maternity/ANC service at registration.
6. Review the information.
7. Save the registration.
8. Confirm the generated patient number.
9. Continue the patient to the appropriate operational workflow.

### Important

Do not create duplicate patient records when the patient already exists. Search the Patient List first where your permissions allow it.

## 5. Patient Dashboard

The Patient Dashboard is the central patient workspace for authorized Front Desk/Clinical users.

Use it to review relevant patient information and the active encounter.

Depending on your permissions, the dashboard may support:

- appointment actions
- vital signs
- clinical information
- services
- prescriptions
- billing-related information
- notes
- patient reporting

Only perform actions for which your account has permission.

## 6. Clinical workflow

Typical clinical flow:

1. Open **Clinical**.
2. Review the Patient List or relevant appointment.
3. Open the patient.
4. Confirm patient identity.
5. Work within the current encounter where applicable.
6. Record clinical information and vital signs.
7. Add required services.
8. Create laboratory/radiology requests where required.
9. Prescribe medicines where appropriate.
10. Admit to Ward/IPD when indicated and authorized.
11. Discharge when the encounter is complete and you have the required permission.

Never alter a patient's clinical record merely to make a report or screen look correct.

## 7. Appointments

1. Open **Clinical → Appointments** where available.
2. Review scheduled appointments.
3. Open the relevant patient.
4. Confirm date, time and reason.
5. Continue the encounter through the appropriate workflow.

## 8. Laboratory

### Viewing requests

1. Open **Laboratory**.
2. Select **Lab Requests**.
3. Locate the patient/request.
4. Confirm the requested test(s).

### Recording results

1. Open the relevant request.
2. Enter the verified result.
3. Review for accuracy.
4. Save/submit the result.
5. Confirm that the result is available to the authorized clinical workflow.

Do not enter laboratory results from memory or assumption.

## 9. Radiology

### Processing a request

1. Open **Radiology**.
2. Select **Radiology Requests**.
3. Locate the patient/request.
4. Perform the required examination according to departmental procedure.

### Recording results

1. Open the relevant request.
2. Enter the verified radiology findings/result.
3. Review the report.
4. Save/submit it.
5. Confirm that the authorized clinical workflow can retrieve it.

## 10. Pharmacy

### Dispensing

1. Open **Pharmacy**.
2. Review the **Dispensing Queue**.
3. Select the patient/prescription.
4. Confirm medicine, quantity and instructions.
5. Verify stock availability.
6. Complete the dispensing/sale workflow.
7. Confirm the transaction is recorded.

### Stock

Use **View Stock** to review current pharmacy stock. Stock adjustments should only be performed by users with the appropriate rights and according to pharmacy stock-control procedures.

## 11. Maternity

Maternity is intended for registered maternity patients.

Available areas include:

- ANC / Labour / PNC
- Antenatal
- Postnatal
- Deliveries
- Admissions
- Reports

For ANC:

1. Ensure the patient is appropriately registered/routed for maternity.
2. Open the maternity workflow.
3. Record the relevant antenatal information.
4. Save each clinical event accurately.
5. Continue follow-up according to the patient's care plan.

## 12. Procurement

Procurement users can work with:

- Suppliers
- Purchase Orders
- Receive Inventory
- Supplier Payables
- Supplier Statements

Typical flow:

**Supplier → Purchase Order → Approval/Processing → Inventory Receipt → Supplier Payable → Statement/Reconciliation**

Always verify quantities and supplier information before completing receiving or financial actions.

## 13. Finance and cashier

Finance users may have access to:

- Central Cashier
- Cashier Shift
- Aged Receivables
- Payment History
- Billing & Invoices
- M-Pesa Payments
- Ledger
- Reconciliation
- Expenses
- Sales Report

### Cashier shift

1. Open **Cashier Shift**.
2. Confirm the active shift.
3. Process authorized patient payments.
4. Verify payment method and amount.
5. Issue/confirm the applicable receipt.
6. Review transactions before closing the shift.

### Payment verification

Never rely solely on a patient-supplied payment message. Use the authoritative HMS payment record and applicable reconciliation process.

## 14. Staff Leave

Staff Leave is a separate Staff function.

Depending on your rights, you may be able to:

- view leave information
- submit a leave request
- perform an authorized leave action/approval

Do not assume access to Administration is required for ordinary Staff Leave use.

## 15. Reports

Use the reports available to your account to review operational activity.

The System Reports area is an Administration function. Department-specific dashboards and reports may be available directly to authorized department users.

For patient reporting, use the Patient Medical Report where authorized.

## 16. Security rules for all users

- Never share your password.
- Never use another employee's account.
- Lock or log out of HMS when leaving a workstation.
- Do not bypass permission checks.
- Do not modify URLs to access restricted pages.
- Verify patient identity before entering clinical information.
- Verify amounts before confirming financial transactions.
- Report suspicious or incorrect records to the appropriate supervisor/administrator.

## 17. Common access messages

### "Forbidden"

Your account does not have the required permission. Contact the HMS administrator.

### "Invalid security token"

Refresh the page and retry. If the problem persists, report it to the technical administrator.

### Information is missing

Do not invent the missing information. Verify the source record or escalate to the responsible department.

## 18. Good practice

The HMS record is an operational record. Every user is responsible for entering accurate information, using the correct patient, following the correct workflow and respecting their assigned permissions.
